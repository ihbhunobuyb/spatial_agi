## Abstract
Robotic manipulation and navigation are fundamental capabilities of embodied intelligence, enabling effective robot interactions with the physical world. Achieving these capabilities requires a cohesive understanding of the environment, including object recognition to localize target objects, object affordances to identify potential interaction areas and spatial affordances to discern optimal areas for both object placement and robot movement. ## Research Focus
- **Domain**: Embodied Intelligence, Robotics
- **Key Topics**: Affordance Learning, Vision-Language Models, Robotic Manipulation, Navigation

## Significance
This paper bridges the gap between high-level VLM capabilities and low-level robotic interactions. By combining object affordances (potential interaction areas) with spatial affordances (optimal placement/navigation areas), it provides a more comprehensive understanding of environment-robot interactions. This is essential for building truly embodied AI systems that can physically interact with and manipulate the world.
