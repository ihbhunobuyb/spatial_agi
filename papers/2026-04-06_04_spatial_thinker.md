## Abstract
Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) have achieved remarkable progress in vision-language tasks, but they continue to struggle with spatial understanding. Existing spatial MLLMs often rely on explicit 3D inputs or architecture-specific modifications, and remain constrained by large-scale datasets or sparse supervision. ## Key Contributions
1. Novel RL-based approach for 3D spatial reasoning in MLLMs
2. Integrates structured spatial grounding with multi-step reasoning
3. Does not require explicit 3D inputs or architecture modifications

## Research Focus
- **Domain**: Multimodal Learning, Spatial Reasoning
- **Key Topics**: 3D Reasoning, Reinforcement Learning, Multimodal LLMs, Spatial Grounding

## Significance
SpatialThinker addresses a key limitation in current MLLMs - the struggle with spatial understanding. Unlike previous approaches that require explicit 3D inputs or architectural changes, SpatialThinker uses RL to learn spatial grounding directly from 2D visual inputs. This makes spatial reasoning more accessible and scalable, potentially enabling broader applications in robotics, AR/VR, and embodied AI.
