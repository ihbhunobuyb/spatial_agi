# Paper Analysis: ViCA - Visuospatial Cognitive Assistant

## Basic Information
- **arXiv ID**: 2505.12312
- **Date**: 2025/05/18
- **Authors**: Feng, Qi (et al.)

## Abstract
Video-based spatial cognition is vital for robotics and embodied AI but challenges current Vision-Language Models (VLMs). This paper makes two key contributions. First, we introduce ViCA (Visuospatial Cognitive Assistant)-322K, a diverse dataset of 322,003 QA pairs from real-world indoor videos (ARKitScenes, ScanNet, ScanNet++), offering supervision for 3D metadata-grounded queries and video-based complex reasoning. ## Key Contributions
1. ViCA-322K dataset: 322,003 QA pairs from real-world indoor videos
2. Uses ARKitScenes, ScanNet, ScanNet++ datasets
3. Provides 3D metadata-grounded queries and video-based reasoning
4. ViCA-7B model achieving state-of-the-art results

## Research Focus
- **Domain**: Video Understanding, Spatial Cognition
- **Key Topics**: Vision-Language Models, 3D Understanding, Video Reasoning, Embodied AI

## Significance
ViCA addresses the challenge of video-based spatial cognition, which is critical for robotics and embodied AI. By creating a large-scale dataset (322K QA pairs) with 3D metadata grounding and developing ViCA-7B, this work provides both data and model resources for advancing spatial reasoning in video understanding. The focus on real-world indoor scenes (ARKitScenes, ScanNet, ScanNet++) ensures practical relevance for home robots and indoor navigation applications.
